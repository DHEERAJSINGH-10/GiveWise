Event Overview

Small actions can create a lasting impact.

This event has concluded. Explore its purpose, key learning areas, and future volunteer opportunities.

This awareness session focuses on the major environmental challenges faced by India, including garbage management, plastic pollution, and cleanliness of public spaces. The session will involve discussions led by the Founder of GiveWise Educational Foundation, volunteers, and core team members, along with interaction with students. Guest speakers working in the field of environmental awareness will also share practical ideas on how small daily actions can create a big impact.

Key Focus Areas

Importance of cleanliness in schools and neighborhoods.

Reducing plastic waste and proper garbage disposal.

Student responsibility in keeping surroundings clean.

Building habits for a cleaner and healthier environment.
