Who We Are

Education is the strongest tool for social change.

GiveWise Educational Foundation is a Section 8 Company registered under the Companies Act, 2013, Government of India.

The foundation is committed to improving quality education by connecting volunteers, educators, mentors, and supporters with children from underserved communities. Through academic support, career guidance, mentorship, and community-driven initiatives, we help students unlock their true potential and build a brighter future.

01 Our Mission

Access, mentorship, opportunity.

To ensure every student has access to quality education, mentorship, and opportunities regardless of their financial background. We strive to empower children through academic excellence, career guidance, skill development, and community support, helping them become confident individuals and responsible citizens.

02 Our Vision

A future where every child can dream freely.

To create a future where every child receives equal educational opportunities and has the confidence to achieve their dreams. We envision a society where education becomes the foundation for social transformation, economic growth, and lifelong success.

Governance & Transparency

Every contribution deserves responsible stewardship.

GiveWise Educational Foundation operates as a non-profit organization with a strong commitment to transparency, accountability, and ethical governance. The Foundation is managed by a Board of Directors in accordance with the Companies Act, 2013. We maintain responsible financial practices and ensure every contribution is utilized efficiently to maximize educational impact.
